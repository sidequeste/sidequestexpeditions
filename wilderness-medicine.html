Wilderness Medicine

Wilderness First Aid & CPR

A hands-on introduction to managing common injuries and medical problems when professional medical care may be delayed or difficult to reach.

The course emphasizes assessment, decision-making, basic treatment skills, and caring for patients in outdoor and remote environments.

Training may include:

  • Patient assessment
  • Basic first aid and injury management
  • Environmental emergencies
  • Medical problems in remote settings
  • CPR fundamentals
  • Emergency planning and decision-making

Emergency Skills

CPR

Learn the essential skills needed to recognize a life-threatening emergency and begin care while emergency medical services are on the way.

This course is designed around practical, hands-on practice so participants leave more comfortable taking action during an emergency.

Training may include:

  • Recognizing cardiac emergencies
  • High-quality CPR fundamentals
  • AED awareness and use
  • Choking response
  • Activating emergency medical services

Emergency Skills

Stop The Bleed

Learn practical techniques for recognizing life-threatening bleeding and taking immediate action before professional medical help arrives.

The course focuses on simple, hands-on skills that can help someone respond quickly and effectively during a serious bleeding emergency.

Training may include:

  • Recognizing severe bleeding
  • Direct pressure
  • Wound packing concepts
  • Tourniquet fundamentals
  • Emergency response priorities
