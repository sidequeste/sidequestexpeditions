Custom Adventures

Custom Adventures give groups the opportunity to build an experience that fits their goals rather than choosing from a fixed itinerary.

Depending on the group, a custom program might focus on outdoor education, leadership, team-building, hiking, camping, backpacking, navigation, paddling, river skills, or simply exploring a remarkable place together.

A custom adventure may include:

  • Day hikes and local exploration
  • Camping and overnight programs
  • Backpacking experiences
  • Outdoor skills instruction
  • Leadership and team development
  • Place-based education and natural history
  • Programs for schools, organizations, or private groups
