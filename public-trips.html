Adventure That Gives Back

Explore With Side Quest

Side Quest Expeditions offers guided outdoor experiences for members of the general public who want to explore incredible places, learn something new, and spend meaningful time outside.

Revenue generated through public trips helps support our nonprofit mission and contributes toward making student outdoor programs more accessible.
