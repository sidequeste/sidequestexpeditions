Our Mission

Adventure With Purpose

Side Quest Expeditions provides San Francisco public school students with professionally led, progressive outdoor education that builds skills, confidence, and community through meaningful experiences outside. We help students carry those experiences forward through continued learning and mentorship, while creating pathways to outdoor employment for those who choose to pursue it. Our public adventures help fund student programming and support a sustainable model for reaching more young people, while helping create an outdoor community that better reflects the communities it serves.

Side Quest Expeditions is a California nonprofit public benefit corporation serving San Francisco public school students.
