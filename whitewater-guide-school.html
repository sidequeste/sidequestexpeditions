Learn The River

In The Guide's Seat

Side Quest Expeditions partners with River Runners USA, a well-regarded American River outfitter known for experienced, highly trained guides and a strong commitment to safety, education, teamwork, and quality river instruction. Together, our guide school programs give participants the opportunity to learn from skilled river professionals while developing the judgment, communication, technical skills, and confidence required to take the guide's seat.

Find Your Course

Youth Program

Youth Guide School

Ages 10–17

A youth-focused introduction to whitewater rafting and guide skills designed to build confidence, teamwork, leadership, and comfort on the river.

Participants will learn through active practice both on and around the water while developing an understanding of how rafting teams communicate and work together.

Participants may explore:

  • Rafting fundamentals and paddle commands
  • River terminology and awareness
  • Boat control and basic maneuvering
  • Team communication
  • River safety concepts
  • Leadership and decision-making

Adult Program

Adult Guide School

Ages 18+

A hands-on introduction to the technical, leadership, and decision-making skills involved in guiding a paddle raft through moving water.

The course is designed for adults who want to become stronger river runners, learn the fundamentals of raft guiding, or gain a deeper understanding of whitewater systems.

Participants may explore:

  • Paddle raft guiding fundamentals
  • Boat angles and river positioning
  • Reading current and identifying hazards
  • Paddle commands and crew management
  • River communication
  • Basic rescue and safety concepts

Oar Rafting

Rowing School

Ages 18+

A practical introduction to maneuvering an oar-powered raft and understanding the fundamentals of rowing in moving water.

This course is intended for adults interested in becoming more capable private boaters, preparing for multi-day river trips, or adding rowing skills to their whitewater experience.

Participants may explore:

  • Oar setup and rowing position
  • Basic strokes and boat control
  • Entering and leaving current
  • Ferry angles and positioning
  • Reading water and choosing lines
  • River safety and trip planning concepts
