Equipment Donations

Help Us Build Our Gear Library

One of our major startup priorities is building an equipment fleet that allows students to participate without having to purchase specialized outdoor gear.

We welcome new equipment as well as appropriate samples, previous-generation products, discontinued inventory, and lightly used gear that remains safe and suitable for program use.
